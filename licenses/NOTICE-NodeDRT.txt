--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  OTHER WORK INSIDE IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Film look presets
      Sets of parameter values, fitted numerically against reference
      renderings produced with spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato, licensed
      CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm
      Each reference is a spectral simulation of one negative stock printed
      on one print stock. NO LUT DATA IS EMBEDDED: what remains are fitted
      parameter values belonging to this transform.
      Full text: SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt

  Subtractive saturation
      Model by Nikita Petrovich, MIT licence. Used by the Chroma control, by
      Mid Purity and by Skin Saturation. It anchors on the strongest channel rather than on
      luminance, so raising saturation lowers the weaker channels instead of
      pushing the strongest one towards clipping — which is what a dye layer
      does, and why a colour that saturates also darkens.
      Full text: MIT-SubtractiveSaturation.txt

  Oklab
      Colour space by Bjorn Ottosson (public domain / MIT), used by Skin Line
      to turn hue. Written here from the published formulas; no code copied.

  OpenFX support library
      Academy Software Foundation, BSD 3-Clause.
      Full text: BSD-3-OpenFX.txt

  Film stock names
      Trademarks of their owners, used descriptively to say which material
      each preset was modelled on. This is an emulation; it is not the stock,
      and it is neither endorsed by nor affiliated with those companies, nor
      with Blackmagic Design.
      Full text: TRADEMARKS.txt
