NODE DRT v2 — what changed (installs next to Node DRT 1.x)
==========================================================

Looks 1-7
  The parametric looks of 1.2 are back (the 1.3 print tables are not used).
  Their curves are rebuilt for the shoulder: no more hard knee near +2 stops
  and clip near +6 - the highlights roll off continuously into white beyond
  +8 stops; about 7 % more contrast at grey; denser blacks on the milky
  looks (Portra, Gold, 400H, 500T). Grey and colour of each look unchanged.

Trims and Color: no colour artifacts in the shadows
  - Shadows: the gain no longer applies below the black floor (negative
    noise was pushed below zero and lit up as colour specks).
  - Log Contrast / Overlay: in the deep shadows they act on the pixel's
    level (neutral lift, single gain) instead of each channel.
  - Color Density / Color Weight: darkening never sends a channel below zero.
  - Brilliance: dosed in stops; at -1 saturated colours no longer drop to
    black and come back.

Density (was Density Pro)
  Show Zones (one zone, or a map of the three) and Show Colors, mutually
  exclusive; Bypass Zones, Bypass Colors; Reset Zones, Reset Density Color.

Skin
  Mask: protected edges (no half-corrected rim around faces), beige walls and
  low-chroma backgrounds left out, pale skin kept; Mask Hue Center (new);
  Invert Mask (Light and Skin Saturation); Skin Line corrects olive skin more
  fully at 1.

Grain: Bypass Grain.

v2 second pass
  Density: the selection is a field blurred in the picture (Mask Softness,
  default 0.5) and re-weighted by each pixel's own colour - no broken edges,
  no halo on neutrals; colour windows fade over at least 18 degrees; the hues
  between two active neighbours are filled; Global Strength x1..x2 with a
  1.75-stop soft ceiling (no more black). Mask Softness and Global Strength at
  the top of the Density section.
  Trims / Contrast: Black Lift (neutral, toe only) and White Roll-Off (soft
  shoulder, white down to 70 %), both monotone and drawn on the Curve View.
  Skin: Show Mask paints the skin selection alone in cyan over a grey picture;
  Mask Edge Softness up to 5x the radius; Invert Mask removed.


NODE_DRT 1.3 — what changed, and where it comes from
====================================================

Performance (same picture)
  - Metal kernels specialised to the settings in use (menus, checkboxes,
    sliders at exactly 0 or 1): default UHD render 57 -> 8 ms on an M2 Pro.
  - Skin mask samples computed once per pixel (GPU pre-pass): 115 -> 17 ms.
  - Grain noise drawn once per pixel (threadgroup memory), cached tables.

Looks 1-7: real prints
  Each look renders through a print: negative, then paper, computed with
  spektrafilm (Andrea Volpato, GPLv3): Vision3 250D / 2383, Vision3 500T /
  2383, Vision3 250D / 2393, Portra 400 / Endura, Ektar 100 / Ultra Endura,
  Pro 400H / Crystal Archive, Gold 200 / Supra Endura. Darkroom choices of
  this plugin: negative developed slightly longer (density curve x1.06), DIR
  couplers x1.15, print flare 2 %, paper white = display white, grey printed
  neutral by the printer lights, grey placed where Default places it, the top
  of the print fading neutral into the paper. One 65^3 table per look, in the
  bundle (Resources/Film); tools/film/gen_film_tables.py rebuilds them.
  HDR: grey follows the HDR rendering, the top of the paper opens up to the
  peak. Nothing is measured from another product.

Grain
  Strength per channel against density and grain kernel per gauge (65, 35,
  16, 8 mm), measured on spektrafilm's grain model at the UHD pixel pitch
  (tools/film/measure_grain.py); independent colour layers; scales
  physically with the frame size. Replaces the 1.2 grain tables.

Density Pro (from RELIEF, by the same author)
  Density by zone and by colour, before Texture and the rendering.

Halation removed from the Inspector.


NODE_ONE — what was modelled, and how
=====================================

This file records what the look presets were fitted against, so the claim in
NOTICE-NodeOne.txt can be checked rather than taken on trust.


The seven film looks
--------------------
Each look is a set of values native to Node One DRT. None of them is a LUT, a
profile, or a copy of anything: each is the result of driving this transform's
own parameters until its OUTPUT matched a reference rendering.

Reference renderings were produced with spektrafilm (Andrea Volpato,
CC BY-SA 4.0), run with auto-exposure off, spatial and stochastic effects off,
and the canonical neutral print settings, so each reference is a fixed colour
transform rather than something that depends on the picture.

    1  Vision3 250D / 2383          kodak_vision3_250d / kodak_2383
    2  Vision3 250D / 2393          kodak_vision3_250d / kodak_2393
    3  Vision3 500T / 2383          kodak_vision3_500t / kodak_2383
    4  Portra 400 / Endura          kodak_portra_400   / kodak_portra_endura
    5  Ektar 100 / Ultra Endura     kodak_ektar_100    / kodak_ultra_endura
    6  Pro 400H / Crystal Archive   fujifilm_pro_400h  / fujifilm_crystal_archive_typeii
    7  Gold 200 / Supra Endura      kodak_gold_200     / kodak_supra_endura

Fitted over 3727 colours spanning a regular grid of the cube, a dense neutral
ramp and a cluster around skin, weighted towards the places the eye judges
first. Error is measured in Oklab.

A fitted look is a deliberate approximation of its reference, not a
reproduction of it.


The "Classic" look
------------------
Obtained the same way, against a reference display rendering rather than a film
one, for people who want that familiar conversion. Fitting against what a
renderer produces is not derivation from that renderer: the output of a program
is not covered by the program's own licence.


The engine's own defaults
-------------------------
Node One DRT's defaults were set by measurement against the same film
references — where skin chroma lands (0.0897, which is film's own value), what
the midtone slope is (0.1448 on film, 0.1473 here), and how chroma falls away
as a source is exposed up. These are measurements of physical materials, which
are facts.


Verification
------------
Every look is checked before it ships, by drt2/check_looks.py: finite output,
inside 0..1, a monotone grey ramp over sixteen stops, and monotone luminance
along twelve colour ramps over five decades. That check has rejected looks
three times; each rejection was a real defect that would have banded on a
gradient.

The shipping kernel is checked against the design reference by drt2/parity.py
over 2000+ colours spanning seven decades, and must agree to float32 precision.


Texture: grain and halation (version 1.1, 28 October 2026)
----------------------------------------------------------
A new group, Texture, with two sub-sections, Grain and Halation, computed by
the plugin itself (src/core/NodeTexture*) on the log image before the display
transform. Both are off by default.

Grain (65mm, 35mm). Per-channel grain strength as a function of the channel's
DaVinci Intermediate value, measured on grey patches; channel correlation
R-G 0.30, R-B 0.25, G-B 0.45; separable spatial kernels per gauge measured at
UHD and scaled with the frame height (the field is point-sampled below UHD,
as measured at HD). Hash noise with a valid convolution, so no tile seams and
identical GPU and CPU results.

Halation. Source = a piecewise-linear curve of the DI-encoded luminance;
spread = a radial kernel stored as six separable terms (4K UHD, scaled with
the frame width, evaluated at half resolution on large frames); added in the
domain T(l) = (l + 0.0482)^0.3635 with layer weights R 1.074, G 0.282,
B 0.100. Only the excess of the surroundings over the pixel's own source is
added. Softness (0 to 1) then diffuses the halo with a gaussian of unit sum
(sigma up to 1.5 x the kernel radius): same total light, smoother profile.

These constants were fitted by measuring film-emulation renders. Only the
numbers are used.


False Color Stops (version 1.2, 28 October 2026)
------------------------------------------------
The False Color overlay now reads the true scene luminance - input decoded by
its log curve, Y row of the input gamut - in stops from 18% grey, in fifteen
one-stop zones from -7 to +7 centred on the whole stops, with a horizontal
numbered legend. Colour palette designed for this plugin. The former reading
(luma of the log code values) is gone.

Base grain made finer and quieter (28 October 2026): Amount 1 / Size 1 now
give 75 % of the measured grain strength at 85 % of its size. The measured
grain read too coarse and too present on real footage.

Highlight Saturation rebuilt (version 1.2, 28 October 2026): it used to nudge
the top of the purity module (+0.011 of chroma at best). It is now a
subtractive saturation (the Chroma model) weighted by the rendered level,
from the upper midtones up: up to about twice the chroma to the right, a
creamy shoulder to the left, white stays white. Checked on the 8 looks, 16
hues over 15 stops: no luminance reversal.

Skin Mask controls (version 1.2, 28 October 2026): Mask Hue Range, Mask
Recovery and Mask Edge Softness, in a new Mask sub-section of Skin. At 0 the
mask is identical to the bit. Checked on 8 looks, 16 hues, 15 stops with the
skin controls at their extremes: no reversal, except 0.3 % on a saturated pink
with Gold 200 / Supra Endura when Hue Range, Recovery and Skin Saturation are
all at maximum - the same order as Chroma at 2.0 already gives.
