Cinematic rendering, right inside DaVinci Resolve.

Node DRT is an OpenFX display rendering transform (DRT). It takes your camera log footage and turns it into a finished, screen-ready image, with the soft highlights and depth of a film render. One node at the end of your tree replaces your output CST and your rendering LUTs. 18% grey stays put on every look, so switching looks never moves your exposure.

Seven film looks

Calibrated against simulations of real negative and print stocks. A continuous highlight roll-off into white, rich contrast, dense blacks. Dialable 0–100%.

Density

Print-style density by zone and by colour, with masks smoothed like a qualifier and smart views of what is selected.

Trims

Log contrast, overlay, Black Lift and White Roll-Off with pivots, shadows, highlight purity and density, brilliance. Curve View on the image.

Skin

A qualifier-style skin matte, Skin Line to the skin tone line, skin saturation and zone-by-zone face lighting.

Film grain

65, 35, 16 and 8mm grain as seen on a 4K scan: fine, organic, mostly luminance.

Never breaks the picture

Every control tested across its full range: no gradient stepping backwards, no colour artifacts in the blacks. False Color, Skin Check, Curve View built in.
